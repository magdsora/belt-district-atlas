# Belt District Atlas

Meningitis cases, alerts and epidemics by health district in the African meningitis belt: WHO's dashboard from 2021, WHO's bulletins 2010-2020 and published outbreak lists from 2003, with WHO's district boundaries for all 26 belt countries.

Live: https://magdsora.github.io/belt-district-atlas/

Sources: WHO meningitis dashboard (cases, alerts and epidemics from 2021); WHO's weekly meningitis bulletins, 2010-2020, extracted by hand by Zhengyang and ZhouZheng; the outbreak list of Cliff and others, 2003-2022 (doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.29900975, CC BY 4.0); WHO polio programme map, the Chad Ministry of Health's district map of 2020 and GADM 4.1 (outlines); background map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
